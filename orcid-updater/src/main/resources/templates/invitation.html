What is a RAiD?

A RAiD (Research Activity Identifier) is a permanent identifier for a research project. It links the project to the people, organisations and outputs involved.

What you are agreeing to

If you continue, you sign in to ORCID. ORCID then asks whether Client may:

  • add and update items in the activities sections of your ORCID record
  • read your ORCID record, including items you have shared only with trusted parties

Client uses this to add this project to your ORCID record as a membership, and to keep it up to date when the project's record changes. You decide at ORCID whether to allow this.
